Delete Your Ghost Money World Account

You can delete your account directly inside the app at any time.

Steps

  1. Open Ghost Money World.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm deletion when prompted.

What is deleted

  • Your user profile/account record.
  • Content records owned by your account, where ownership is available.
  • Profile media uploaded under your account storage path.

Data retention

Account deletion is processed immediately. Some technical backups may remain for a limited period and are automatically removed per platform retention policy.
